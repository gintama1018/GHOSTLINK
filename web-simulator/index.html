Brutal Honesty: The Physics of Zero-RF

GhostLink trades RF interception (WiFi/Bluetooth sniffing) for optical line-of-sight and acoustic listening surfaces. F7 Payload Encryption (AES-GCM-256) closes this gap so visual or audio recordings yield only ciphertext. Optical achieves ~1.0–2.5 KB/s; Ultrasonic achieves ~0.02–0.1 KB/s (128 KB safety cap); Magnetic contact transfers 8 bytes in ~8–12 seconds.
